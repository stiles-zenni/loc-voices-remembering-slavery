Interview with unidentified former slaves from Virginia, Petersburg, Virginia, ca. 1937-1940. The Library of Congress's transcript, which keeps the language of the recording. Times link to that moment in the audio. [unclear] marks speech the transcriber could not make out; italics, their best guess. The timing for this recording is approximate.

Hampton Institute Duplication Project (AFC 1948/015), American Folklife Center, Library of Congress. View at the Library of Congress.

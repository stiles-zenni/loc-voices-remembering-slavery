Notes on Language

Words from the recordings, in context

These are words and phrases the speakers use to describe slavery and its end, with a little of their history. Wherever possible, each links to a moment where you can hear it said. Counts are across all sixty transcripts in the Library's collection.

Some entries describe violence under slavery. Links open the interview paused at that moment; nothing plays until you choose.
